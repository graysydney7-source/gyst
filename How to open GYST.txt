🎀 GYST — get your shit together

1. Drag GYST into the Applications folder (the shortcut right next to it).
2. Open Applications and double-click GYST.

The first time, your Mac may say GYST "can't be opened" or "Apple could not
verify" it. That's only because it was made at home, not bought from the App Store.

To allow it:
  • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Scroll down to the message about GYST and click "Open Anyway"
  • Enter your Mac password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again

(On older macOS versions you can instead right-click GYST → Open → Open.)

You only have to do this once.

Your data stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/GYST
(with an automatic daily backup). Export a backup any time from the
settings button at the bottom of the sidebar.
